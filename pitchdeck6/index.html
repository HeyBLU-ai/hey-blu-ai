Competitive Landscape

The Competition Gap

  • Pro Systems ($500K+): Too expensive for youth leagues
  • Consumer Apps: 2D tracking only, no 3D spatial zones
  • Sports Platforms: Focus on scoring/stats, not officiating

Our Unique Position

  • Pro-level accuracy: Consumer device and pricing.
  • Spatial Telemetry: 3D tracking, no sensors required.
  • First Officiating app: Baseball first mover advantage.

Market Risks & Mitigation

Umpire Resistance

"Human element" preference vs. objective calls. Mitigation: Free tool, optional use, confidence building.

Distribution Pivot

GameChanger's 1M+ user base could pivot to officiating. Mitigation: 2-year patent head start, specialized focus.

Tech Incumbents

TrackMan/RECON could target amateur market. Mitigation: Consumer pricing impossible at their cost structure.
