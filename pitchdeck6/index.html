APPENDIX A: Competitive Landscape

Strategic Moat

Cannibalization Risk

A company selling a $5,000 radar unit has no incentive to offer a $99/year software solution that accomplishes the core job for a fraction of the price.

Brand Promise

Their brands are built on delivering professional-grade, sub-millimeter accuracy. A phone-based solution would dilute that hard-won brand equity.

Go-to-Market Mismatch

Their sales channels focus on professional teams, elite academies, and facility owners - not the fragmented world of youth leagues and volunteer umpires.

Technical Debt

Their software and algorithms are built around multi-view geometry and radar data. Retooling for on-device, monocular computer vision is a non-trivial engineering effort.

BLU's Strategic Advantage

These structural barriers create a significant moat, giving BLU time to establish market dominance in the amateur baseball officiating space before incumbents can effectively respond.

APPENDIX C: Product & User Experience

The Gameday Workflow

Before Game

Umpire Setup: Mounts phone on fence, completes 60-second field scan to calibrate strike zone

Team Connection: Coaches and parents connect via simple QR code

During Game

Umpire: Receives optional, discrete audio tones in earbud confirming calls or offering suggestions on close pitches

Coach: Views live, updating pitch map on their own device from the dugout

Family: Follows along with basic stats from the stands

After Game

Umpire: Exports incident reports within minutes

Coach: Accesses Pro analytics for detailed review

Family: Receives shareable Player Game Card

Design Philosophy

Invisible until needed: BLU integrates naturally into the flow of a game, providing assistance only when required without disrupting the traditional baseball experience.

APPENDIX D: Core Technology & IP Protection

Patent Application Details

Publication Number: US 2025/0281795 A1

Title: Training and Inference of an Automated Machine Learning Model for Detecting Position of a Moving Object Relative to a Reference Object in a Sporting or Other Event

Applicant: Big League Ballpark Inc.

Filing Date: March 7, 2025

Priority Claimed: Based on provisional applications filed as early as March 8, 2024

Patent Diagram

Single-device system using off-the-shelf camera for ball-strike detection

What Our Patent Protects

Single-Device System

The method for both training and implementing a machine learning model for calling balls and strikes using only a single, off-the-shelf camera (e.g., an iPhone).

Why it Matters: Eliminates expensive multi-camera systems, making professional-grade officiating accessible to amateur leagues.

2D-to-3D Inference

The system's ability to be trained using only two-dimensional (2D) image data to then infer the 3D position of the ball relative to the strike zone.

Why it Matters: Revolutionary approach that eliminates the need for 3D training data, dramatically reducing development complexity and cost while maintaining accuracy.

End-to-End Officiating Logic

The complete workflow including:

• Initial Field Calibration: 60-second setup process

• 3D Strike Zone Calculation: Dynamic zone orientation

• Ball/Strike Determination: Real-time decision making

Why it Matters: Protects the entire user experience from setup to final call, preventing competitors from implementing similar workflows.

Strategic Value

Our comprehensive patent-pending system covers the fundamental processes that make HeyBLU uniquely viable for the amateur market, establishing a significant technical and commercial moat that de-risks investment and secures first-mover advantage.
