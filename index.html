How It Works

One phone, one tripod—that’s the setup.

1

Set sport & zone

Pick youth baseball or softball and match the zone to your league.

2

Mount the iPhone

Tripod beside home plate with a clear view of the plate and pitching lane.

3

Lock the plate

Tap to anchor the zone to real home plate, then fine-tune alignment.

4

Throw

Hear ball/strike calls out loud on every pitch. Live zone feedback as you go.

5

Review

Open heatmaps and pitch data when the session ends—ready to share.
