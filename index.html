How It Works

1

Set sport & zone

Choose baseball or softball, and adjust the strike zone size to match your league.

2

Mount the iPhone

Place a tripod beside home plate with a clear view of the plate and pitching lane (foul-line / on-deck alignment).

3

Lock the plate

Tap the screen to anchor the 3D plate to the real home plate, then fine-tune on Mount & Verify.

4

Throw

Get real-time audio calls and live zone feedback on every pitch. Review heatmaps and session data when the bullpen ends.

5

Review

Open heatmaps and pitch data as soon as the session ends—on your phone, ready to share.

Pitchers take private lessons, but instructors rarely get to see how they perform during the week. HeyBLU's exportable Session Reports and Baseball Cards make it easy to text objective results. Instructors get real data between lessons to keep a player's development on track.
Coaches manually track pitches on clipboards to protect arms and strictly adhere to league pitch-limit rules. HeyBLU automatically logs every tracked pitch during a live session. This removes the administrative headache and ensures you have a perfectly accurate pitch count when the inning ends.
Pitchers often throw for multiple teams or do side work, making it difficult to track their total weekly volume. HeyBLU saves a pitcher's history directly to the device. Parents and coaches can monitor aggregate pitch counts to prevent overuse and safely manage a young player's workload.
Coaches in the dugout need to know if the relief pitcher warming up in the bullpen is actually finding the zone. By syncing an iPad or second iPhone at the bullpen, coaches can monitor pitch locations remotely in real-time. You will know exactly when your reliever is dialed in and ready for the mound.
Coaches already run "Knockout" or simulated at-bats to keep practice competitive and fun. Instead of a coach standing dangerously behind the plate acting as both umpire and catcher, HeyBLU makes the objective call instantly. This frees the coach to step back, run the drill, and focus entirely on player mechanics while the app handles the strike zone.
Teams already run scrimmages with a coach calling balls and strikes from the field. Mount HeyBLU at the backstop for consistent zone feedback while you coach bases and defense. Leagues scaling automated balls and strikes for multiple fields can talk to us about ABS for your organization—the same app families use in the bullpen.
A great catcher steals strikes, but a coach needs to know if the pitch was actually in the zone. HeyBLU calls the true location of the ball as it crosses the plate, regardless of where the glove ends up. If the app calls a ball but the catcher makes it look like a strike, coaches can use that objective feedback to discuss, score, and refine framing techniques.
Volunteer umpires face immense pressure from the sidelines and have few tools to improve their zone consistency. Umpire associations can use HeyBLU as a training aid during scrimmages or cage work. Umpires can mentally call the pitch and instantly hear the app's objective ruling to calibrate their eye.
It is impossible to remember where every pitch went after a 45-pitch bullpen. HeyBLU automatically generates a visual heatmap of the entire session. Coaches and players can instantly spot trends, mechanical flaws, or cold zones that need work.
Serious players and teams need actionable data to review over a season. HeyBLU allows users to export detailed pitch logs and performance cards. This data can be analyzed to track progress or sent to scouts to prove a pitcher's command development over time.
