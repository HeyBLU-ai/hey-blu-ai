Big League Ballpark Inc., doing business as HeyBLU ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use our website (heyblu.ai), our strike zone tracking application, rulebook assistant, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information from you:

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Account information and preferences
  • Information you provide when using our rulebook assistant (questions asked, league preferences)
  • Usage data from our strike zone tracking application (pitch data, game analytics)
  • Device information and technical data (IP address, browser type, device identifiers)
  • Information about your use of our site (via cookies or analytics tools)
  • Payment information (if applicable, processed securely by third-party payment processors)

2. How We Collect Information

We collect information in the following ways:

  • When you fill out forms (e.g., to join the beta waitlist, request league rulebook integration, or contact us)
  • When you use our Services (e.g., asking questions in the rulebook assistant, using strike zone tracking)
  • Through cookies and third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • When you voluntarily provide information through surveys, feedback forms, or customer support

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Services
  • Process beta program applications and manage waitlists
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • Send you important updates about our Services, product launches, and beta program
  • Analyze usage patterns and improve user experience
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share your data only with trusted third-party service providers, such as:

  • OpenAI, to power our AI rulebook assistant and strike zone analysis
  • Cloud hosting providers (e.g., Vercel), to host our Services
  • Database providers (e.g., Neon, PostgreSQL), to store and manage data
  • Payment processors (if applicable), to securely process payments
  • Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics), to track and analyze site usage
  • Email service providers, to manage communications

These services are only provided access to data necessary to perform their functions and are obligated to keep your information confidential.

We may also disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your information, including encryption, secure servers, and access controls. However, no system can guarantee absolute security, and you use our Services at your own risk.

6. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Correct or update your data
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA. Please contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your use of our Services. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.
