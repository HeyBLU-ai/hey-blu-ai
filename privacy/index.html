Big League Ballpark Inc., doing business as HeyBLU ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. The HeyBLU strike zone app processes camera input on your device in real time; it does not upload or store game video on our servers.

1. Information We Collect

Local processing guarantee. HeyBLU is built on a privacy-forward architecture. The app processes camera frames locally on your device in real-time to track pitches and generate strike zone data. We do not transmit, upload, store, or retain video recordings or images off-device.

HeyBLU iOS App (Core Use)

You can use the core strike zone tracking features without providing personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name or email address. The app does not require an account to run a session on your device.

During on-device operation, the app may generate and keep on your iPhone:

  • Pitch and session data (e.g., ball/strike calls, pitch counts, speeds, locations, timestamps)
  • Setup and calibration values you enter (e.g., strike zone size, distance, device role)
  • Session summaries and exports you choose to save or share from your device

Camera frames are processed in real time on-device for tracking. They are not saved as video files by HeyBLU and are not uploaded to our servers as part of normal app operation.

HeyBLU Score

HeyBLU Score is a free scorebook. Games, rosters, and settings stay on your device. There is no account, no camera, and no subscription. We do not receive your scorebook unless you email us or choose to send a file (for example AirDrop, Messages, or a CSV/PDF you export). HeyBLU Score does not use PostHog, RevenueCat, or the camera/AR features described above for the strike zone app.

Identity, Children, and Analytics

  • No PII required for core app use: Name, email, and similar identifiers are not required to track pitches in the app.
  • Minors: HeyBLU may be used around youth baseball, but we do not link a child's on-field activity to a child's identity in our systems. We do not knowingly collect a minor's name or contact information through the core app.
  • App analytics and diagnostics: The HeyBLU strike zone app may send pseudonymous data (not your name or email)—such as a device identifier, product interaction events, crash or performance signals, and subscription-related status—to help us understand how the app is used and keep it stable. We use PostHog for product analytics and Apple developer tools for crash and performance diagnostics. These signals are not used to profile individual players on the field. HeyBLU Score does not include this analytics.
  • On-device AR / camera: Environment scanning and camera frames used for strike-zone tracking (e.g., via ARKit) are processed on your device for the session. We do not upload live camera video or images from normal app operation to PostHog or our servers.

Website, Beta, and Support (When You Contact Us)

If you use heyblu.ai or reach out to us, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Name and contact information (e.g., email) when you join the beta waitlist, submit a form, or email support
  • Daily 20 contest: adult email address, Wall of Fame display name, heatmap image, rules acceptance, and optional marketing opt-in when you enter on heyblu.ai/daily20. Email is used to administer the contest (including winner notification). Marketing / product news is sent only if you check the optional marketing box. The public Wall of Fame shows display name and heatmap only—not your email. See the Daily 20 Official Rules.
  • Information you provide in support or feedback messages (device model, iOS version, steps to reproduce, optional logs)
  • Questions and preferences if you use a text-based rulebook assistant on the website
  • Website usage data via cookies or analytics on heyblu.ai (see Section 8)
  • Payment information only if you make a purchase, handled by the app store or payment processor (we do not store full card numbers)

If you optionally attach diagnostic files to a support request, we use them only to investigate that issue. We do not treat optional support attachments as part of the app's normal data collection.

2. How We Collect Information

We collect information in the following ways:

  • When you fill out forms (e.g., to join the beta waitlist, enter Daily 20, request league rulebook integration, or contact us)
  • When you use our Services (e.g., asking questions in the rulebook assistant, using strike zone tracking)
  • Through cookies and third-party analytics on the website (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Through in-app product analytics (PostHog) and subscription status services (RevenueCat / Apple), using pseudonymous identifiers and purchase-related status—not your payment card number
  • When you voluntarily provide information through surveys, feedback forms, or customer support

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Services
  • Run on-device strike zone tracking, pitch logging, and session summaries on your iPhone
  • Administer website promotions such as Daily 20 (winner notification; marketing only with separate opt-in)
  • Process beta program applications and manage waitlists
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • Send important updates about our Services, product launches, and beta program (when you have provided contact information)
  • Measure product usage with PostHog (pseudonymous device and interaction data) and diagnose crashes or performance issues using Apple developer tools
  • Verify subscription status and entitlements via Apple and RevenueCat
  • Analyze website usage patterns and improve user experience
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Subscriptions & Payments

HeyBLU is a paid auto-renewable subscription purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not receive or store your payment card number. Apple processes payment. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status and entitlements (such as whether a subscription is active, in trial, or expired).

What Apple / RevenueCat may share with us

  • Subscription status and entitlement (active, trial, expired, or cancelled)
  • Product identifier, purchase date, and renewal period (monthly or annual)
  • Anonymous or pseudonymous transaction identifiers used for support and fraud prevention

What we do not receive from Apple

  • Full payment card numbers or bank account details
  • Your Apple ID password

Apple's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. For subscription management and cancellation, use Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device, or visit Apple's subscription management page.

Subscription terms, including auto-renewal, optional free trials or promotional offers, pricing, and cancellation, are described in our Terms of Service & EULA.

Subscription vs Follow Game data

HeyBLU subscribers may have session summaries saved to Session History on the device for later review. Follow Game viewing on a second device does not persist session data after the connection ends. We do not upload game video from the HeyBLU camera to our servers as part of normal app operation.

5. Sharing of Information and Third-Party Processors

We do not sell your personal data. We do not upload or store HeyBLU camera video or images on our servers as part of normal app operation.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us operate the website, beta program, and app distribution. They are obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

Third-Party Processors

Depending on how you interact with us, the following services may process data:

  • Apple (Apple Inc.): App Store, TestFlight, In-App Purchase, and iOS platform services (including crash and performance diagnostics you allow through iOS). Subject to Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/privacy/privacy-policy
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.): Subscription status, entitlements, and related purchase history signals for the iOS app. Subject to RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • PostHog (PostHog Inc.): Product analytics for the iOS app (e.g., pseudonymous device identifiers and product interaction events). Subject to PostHog's privacy policy: https://posthog.com/privacy
  • Vercel (Vercel Inc.): Hosts the heyblu.ai website. Subject to Vercel's privacy policy: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Formspree (or similar form handlers): Processes information you submit through website forms (e.g., beta signup, support). Subject to the processor's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C.), if enabled: Processes text-only queries for an optional rulebook assistant on the website—not live camera video from the iOS app. Subject to OpenAI's privacy policy: https://openai.com/privacy
  • Website analytics: We may use analytics on heyblu.ai (e.g., Google Analytics) that collect pseudonymous or aggregated website usage data.

What We Do Not Share From Normal App Use

  • Live camera video streams or recorded game video from HeyBLU sessions
  • Biometric data derived from video
  • Photos or image files captured by the app for cloud storage

Third-Party Processor Requirements

Service providers we use are expected to:

  • Use your data only for the purposes we specify
  • Implement appropriate security measures
  • Comply with applicable privacy laws and regulations
  • Delete or return your data when the processing relationship ends (subject to legal retention requirements)

We may also disclose your information if required by law, to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users, or in connection with a business transfer (e.g., merger, acquisition, or sale of assets).

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (using TLS/SSL) for website and support communications
  • Secure hosting and infrastructure with access controls for data you send us voluntarily
  • On-device processing for core strike zone tracking so camera frames stay on your iPhone during normal use
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Limited access to personal data we hold on a need-to-know basis

However, no system can guarantee absolute security, and you use our Services at your own risk. We encourage you to use strong passwords and keep your account credentials secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law.

On Your Device

Session and pitch data from the HeyBLU strike zone app, and games and rosters in HeyBLU Score, are stored on your device unless you export or share them yourself. You can delete that data in the app. Uninstalling an app removes its locally stored data from that device.

We do not retain copies of live camera video or images from normal app operation on our servers.

Data You Send to Us

  • Beta and contact forms: Name, email, and form responses—typically up to 3 years after your last interaction, or sooner if you request deletion.
  • Daily 20 entries: Adult email, display name, heatmap image, and consent flags—retained as needed to run the promotion and contact winners, and for marketing only if you opted in; request deletion via support@heyblu.ai.
  • Support communications: Emails, bug reports, and optional attachments you send—typically up to 3 years to resolve issues and improve the product.
  • Website analytics: Pseudonymous or aggregated usage data—typically up to 2 years.
  • App product analytics (PostHog): Pseudonymous device and interaction data—retained according to our PostHog project settings and PostHog's standard periods.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics: Pseudonymous technical logs via Apple's developer tools—retained according to Apple's standard periods.
  • Subscription status (RevenueCat / Apple): Entitlement and purchase-history signals needed to provide the subscription—retained while relevant to an active or recently active subscription and per those vendors' policies.
  • Legal and compliance: We may retain certain records longer if required by law, regulation, dispute resolution, or to protect our legal rights.

Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information we hold about you (forms, support, or marketing contact), email support@heyblu.ai. We will respond within a reasonable time. Deletion of on-device session data is controlled by you in the app or by removing the app from your device.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request deletion of your personal information

  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA. Please contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your use of our Services. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect functionality.

10. Children's Privacy

HeyBLU is often used around youth baseball, but the app is operated by coaches, parents, leagues, or other adults. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) where it applies to information collected online.

We do not link a minor's on-field pitch data to that minor's identity in our systems. The core app does not require a child to register with name or email. Any PII we receive is typically from an adult (for example, a beta signup or support email).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information through a website form or email, contact support@heyblu.ai to review, modify, or delete it.
