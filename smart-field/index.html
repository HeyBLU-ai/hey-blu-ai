Can HeyBLU be used in games?

Yes—as an umpire aide, not a replacement. We haven’t game-tested it enough to call it set-and-forget. If your goal is better, more consistent ball/strike calls, it can work when someone owns the system and follows the practices we recommend.

We’re looking for leagues, travel orgs, and fields ready to pilot with us. We know this system inside and out—email us and we’ll walk you through how it might work for your crew.

What game use actually requires

  • Two phones. One tracks pitches. The second runs calibrate and Command Center—pause during warm-ups, pre-game setup, mid-game re-scans, rosters, and fixing a bad call without touching the tracking phone.
  • A dedicated owner. Someone is responsible for accuracy. That can mean a fresh scan between innings, adjusting the zone for a batter, or keeping the roster straight. It isn’t “mount it and walk away.”
  • Earbuds for the umpire. The plate ump hears the assist; they still own swings, runners, and the rest of the game.

Who this is for

Leagues and orgs that want more consistent calls from their umpires—and understand this takes a person on the system, not a magic box.
