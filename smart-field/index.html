Leagues · UICs · field operators

A smart field starts with one iPhone

HeyBLU anchors ball/strike feedback to home plate in 3D—live audio, pitch location, and session data from a mounted iPhone. Add a second device when your crew wants a bench view or remote calibration without touching the tripod phone.

We built HeyBLU for outdoor bullpens and practice. Leagues and UICs who want to explore scrimmages, fall ball, or structured in-game pilots are welcome—we will walk through mount, crew workflow, and what “assist” means at your level. We do not promise turnkey MLB-style ABS on day one; we help serious operators get there step by step.

Two roles, one field

Primary · Track Game

Tripod iPhone

Scan home plate, mount in foul territory (10–15 ft ideal), verify alignment, Play Ball. Live ball/strike audio for taken pitches. Human umpire still owns swings, runners, and game flow.

Secondary · Follow Game

Bench or press box

Read-only live mirror over local Wi‑Fi—no AR on this device. UIC, league admin, or parent volunteer watches pitch events as they happen.

Secondary · Calibrate BLU

Control tower

Password-gated remote influence: zone resize, plate nudge, manual call overlay, pitch delete, Command Training intent. Primary device stays authoritative; corrections are for QA and training workflows.

What a smart field gives you

  • Plate-anchored 3D strike zone — not a flat video overlay; geometry locked to the real dish.
  • Live ball/strike audio — instant feedback; many umpires use Bluetooth earbuds so only the plate ump hears the assist.
  • Pitch location logging — every tracked pitch captured for review, export, and umpire training.
  • Operator tools — pause/resume, delete last pitch, active pitcher tagging, session exports (Pro).
  • Honest limits — swings stay with the human; outdoor daylight is the happy path; setup takes a few minutes, not seconds.

Suggested path for leagues

  1. 1.Start in bullpens and umpire zone clinics—UIC and coaches download the app and run the Field Guide setup together.
  2. 2.Add a second device for Follow or Calibrate when your crew is comfortable with mount and scan rhythm.
  3. 3.Pilot scrimmages or fall ball when stakeholders agree on mount placement, scan cadence, zone size, and who hears audio.
