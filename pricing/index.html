Common questions

Is there a free trial?
Yes. HeyBLU includes a 14-day free trial on monthly and annual plans. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions to avoid being charged.
Does Follow Game need a subscription?
No. Non-subscribers can download the app to follow along from their own devices for free when a subscriber allows access.
What is launch pricing?
$4.99/month or $49.99/year after the 14-day trial. The App Store shows the price that applies when you subscribe.
How do I subscribe or cancel?
Subscribe in the app via the App Store. Manage or cancel anytime under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. See our Terms of Service.
League or tournament use?
HeyBLU is built for bullpens and practice. If your goal is live-game ball-strike calls, let us know how we can help—info@heyblu.ai.
