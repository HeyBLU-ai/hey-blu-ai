Common questions

What counts toward the 50-pitch weekly cap?
Any pitch you save to session history, export, or share—including branded share cards. Live ball/strike audio and the on-screen zone view during an active session are unlimited and never count toward the cap.
When does my weekly count reset?
Your saved-pitch counter resets each week. The exact reset timing is shown in the app.
Is there a free trial for Pro?
The free tier is the trial—50 saved pitches per week with unlimited live feedback. Pro unlocks unlimited saves and advanced tools when you are ready.
