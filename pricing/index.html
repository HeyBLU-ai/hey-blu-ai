Common questions

Is there a free trial?
Yes. HeyBLU Pro includes a 14-day free trial on monthly and annual plans. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions to avoid being charged.
Do I need HeyBLU Pro for live ball/strike calls?
Yes, on the tracking phone. Track Pitches — live audio, pitch location, and estimated speed — requires an active HeyBLU Pro subscription (trial counts). Follow Game on a second device is free to watch when someone with Pro is running the session.
What is launch pricing?
HeyBLU Pro is available at an introductory rate of $4.99/month or $49.99/year after the free trial. Standard pricing may apply to new subscribers later; the App Store always shows the price that applies at purchase.
Can I share a heatmap without Pro?
On Follow Game, you can share a heatmap from the session report at no charge. Full tracking sessions, unlimited exports, and Session History require HeyBLU Pro.
How do I subscribe or cancel?
Subscribe in the app via the App Store. Manage or cancel anytime under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. See our Terms of Service.
League or tournament use?
HeyBLU is optimized for bullpens and practice. League pilots are invite-only—info@heyblu.ai.
