The Problem

According to the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS), nearly 80% of officials quit within their first two years, largely due to verbal abuse from parents and coaches. This trend is creating a critical shortage across all youth sports.

❌

Game Cancellations

Leagues are increasingly forced to cancel or reschedule games due to a lack of certified umpires.

⚠️

Inconsistent Officiating

Parents and coaches with no training are often forced to officiate, leading to inconsistent strike zones and rising on-field disputes.

🔄

High Turnover

The high-pressure, low-support environment makes it nearly impossible to recruit and retain new umpires, worsening the cycle.

4/10

The Solution

HeyBLU is an AI-powered iPhone app that gives umpires a perfect view of the strike zone. It acts as a co-pilot, not a replacement, delivering discreet, real-time ball-and-strike calls via an earbud. The umpire remains in complete control, backed by objective data.

⚡

Instant & Objective

On-device AI delivers calls instantly, removing human bias from the most contentious part of the game.

🚀

Effortless Setup

Setup in seconds: position your tripod, perform a brief field scan with the app, and press play.

👥

A Tool to Recruit & Retain Umpires

By reducing conflict and providing a powerful, objective training tool, BLU aims to solve the core reasons why 80% of new officials quit.

Key Features & Technology

  • •
    Adaptive Strike Zone: AI automatically adjusts the strike zone for each batter's height and stance.
  • •
    On-Device Processing: Core ball/strike calls run entirely on the iPhone, requiring no Wi-Fi or cell service for in-game use. Premium analytics like heatmaps sync to the cloud post-game.
  • •
    Privacy by Design: No video is sent to the cloud, ensuring total privacy for minors.
