Review Cockpit

Enter your admin password to inspect and correct ingested rulebook atoms.

1
Check Duplicates
Look at the Duplicate Rule #s metric card. If it's red, filter by that rule number and confirm the duplicate atoms aren't accidentally merging two distinct rules — they should share the same parent section.
2
Filter In-Game Rules
Toggle Show unnumbered only. Skim the list and mentally skip administrative atoms (fees, roster limits, trading deadlines, jersey requirements). Those do not need a rule number.
3
Fix Critical Rules
Use the search bar to hunt for critical mechanics by keyword: collision · slide · interference · obstruction · ejection · appeal. These are the atoms that must have a proper citation number.
4
Update & Save
Click the ✏️ pencil icon on an unnumbered row. Look up the correct parent rule number in the source PDF. Type it in and press Enter or click ✓ Save. The row updates instantly.
5
Ignore the Rest
Leave glossary definitions, appendix items, and administrative bullets as unnumbered. Only in-game mechanics that an umpire or coach would cite during a game need a rule number.
