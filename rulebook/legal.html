About HeyBLU.AI

HeyBLU.AI is an educational tool designed to help baseball and softball participants understand rules and resolve disputes quickly and accurately. Our goal is to provide instant access to rule interpretations and guidance to reduce arguments and improve the game experience for everyone involved.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This application provides rule interpretations and guidance but is not an official rulebook. All interpretations are for educational purposes only and should not replace official rulebooks or umpire decisions. In case of disputes, the official rulebook and umpire decisions take precedence.

Official Rule Sources

For complete, authoritative, and up-to-date rules, please refer to these official sources:

Major League Baseball (MLB)

  • Official Rulebook: MLB Official Rules
  • Rule Interpretations: MLB Rule Interpretations

Little League International

  • Official Rules: Little League Official Rules
  • Rulebook App: Available on iOS and Android

USSSA (United States Specialty Sports Association)

  • Official Rules: USSSA Baseball Rules
  • Tournament Rules: USSSA Tournament Rules

Local League Rules

Many local leagues have additional rules or modifications to the standard rulebooks. Always check with your local league officials for any league-specific rules that may apply.
