Games, scrimmages & the umpire’s full job

After MLB’s ABS rollout, every level of baseball is asking what objective zone feedback could look like on their field. HeyBLU brings stadium-grade plate-anchored 3D tracking down to a single iPhone on the backstop — real ball flight in real time, processed on the device.

What HeyBLU does at the plate

  • Live Strike/Ball audio when the batter takes the pitch — instant feedback for everyone on the field.
  • Pitch crossing location logged on swings too — the zone dot and session data still capture where the ball crossed the plate when the trajectory is trusted.
  • 3D geometry locked to home plate via ARKit — the zone moves with the real dish, not a flat overlay on video.

What the plate umpire still owns

A home-plate umpire is running a small courtroom every pitch: checked swings, catcher’s interference, balks, time, runners, equipment, and the rhythm of the game. HeyBLU focuses on one hard problem well — where the baseball crossed the strike zone in 3D — so humans can stay in charge of everything else.

That makes HeyBLU a strong umpire training companion: call the pitch, hear HeyBLU’s feedback, compare, adjust. Same for parent-umpires learning the zone at 10U and 12U.
