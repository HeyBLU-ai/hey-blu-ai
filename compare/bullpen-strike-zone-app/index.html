Bullpen strike zone app — what coaches actually need

Travel-ball dads and pitching coaches run hundreds of bullpen pitches a month. You want the hitter’s eye view: was it a strike? Not a spreadsheet three hours later.

Two different “bullpen apps”

  1. Analytics behind the pitcher — velocity, spin, video tracers, zone heatmaps after the fact (PitchLab, Ember). Camera near release or arm-side. Great for pitch design; no live umpire call.
  2. Assistive caller at the plate — HeyBLU mounts at the backstop in foul territory, anchors the zone to the real plate, and speaks Strike or Ball on taken pitches.

Honest envelope

  • Outdoor daylight (primary supported path)
  • iPhone 11+ on a sturdy tripod
  • ~10–15 ft from plate in foul territory
  • Taken pitches only — no swing/check-swing calls
  • Not rated for indoor cages or dusk
