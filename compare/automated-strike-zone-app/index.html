Automated strike zone apps — what to expect

Search “automated strike zone app” and you will find pitch trackers, umpire clickers, charting tools, and stadium tech. Most results do not give you a live Strike or Ball call. Here is a simple map.

Stadium / portable hardwareMLB ABS, Magnus RECONLeague / lab gear — not a phone app

HeyBLU in one sentence

Mount an iPhone on a tripod in foul territory, lock the zone to home plate, and hear objective ball/strike calls on taken pitches — built for outdoor youth bullpens, not a $10k camera rig.

  • iOS only · outdoor daylight primary
  • Regulation baseball · tripod required
  • Assistive training — not a replacement for a certified game umpire
  • Silent when the app is not confident in the trajectory (by design)
