iPhone Pro: blue zone jumped when you mounted?

You didn’t do anything wrong. Pro phones are mismeasuring. Your own measurements fix it.

  1. Enter the real distance to the plate and height from the ground (tape measure).
  2. Slide the blue plate onto the real home plate with your finger.
  3. Confirm placement using Adjust Plate, then Play Ball.

Follow Game or Command Center won’t connect?

Fast fix: Android hotspot or travel router—both iPhones join it. Not every Wi‑Fi works. The tracking phone cannot host its own hotspot and may need to be on airplane mode.
