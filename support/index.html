How to scan

Tips to collect high-quality video for consistent tracking.

  1. Lighting: Even front lighting, avoid bright backlight behind the plate.
  2. Mounting: Use a tripod or steady mount. Aim for a 10–20 foot distance (adjust by field layout).
  3. Orientation: Landscape recommended; keep the plate fully visible.
  4. Settings: Use 1080p @ 60fps if available. Avoid low-light & high ISO.
  5. Sample video: If tracking fails, record a short 10–20s clip showing the issue to attach to your report.

Placing the strike zone

Correct strike zone placement is essential for accurate calls.

  • Center the strike zone on the plate horizontally.
  • Resize to match batter height — top near chest, bottom near knees (or according to your league rules).
  • Rotate only if the camera is tilted — keep the zone axis aligned with the plate edges.
Examples
Too large: includes catcher & background. Too small: clips the plate. Off-center: misses high/low pitches.

Troubleshooting — When it isn't tracking

Run this checklist in order — most issues are resolved in steps 1–4.

  1. Permissions — Confirm Camera, Microphone, and Motion sensors are allowed (Settings → HeyBLU).
  2. Lighting & background — Move to even light and reduce background clutter behind the plate.
  3. Framing — Ensure the plate is fully visible and the strike zone is correctly placed.
  4. Device health — Close background apps, free space, reboot, reinstall if necessary.
  5. Update — Make sure you're on the latest TestFlight build.
  6. Sample test — Try a known-good sample video. If that works, contrast with your setup to find the difference.
  7. Network — If cloud processing is required, ensure stable internet.
  8. Capture diagnostics — Record a 10–30s clip showing the issue and describe steps to reproduce (see Report a bug).

TestFlight & Beta testers

How to join, update and report crashes via TestFlight.

  1. Open the TestFlight invite link and install the app.
  2. To update: open TestFlight → tap Update.
  3. To share a crash: open TestFlight → App → Share Beta Feedback or send diagnostics from within the app.
  4. Include device model, iOS version, TestFlight build number and a short reproduction video.
