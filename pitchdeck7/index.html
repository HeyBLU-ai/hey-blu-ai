Adoption flywheel

  1. Free co‑pilot → more covered games.
  2. More games → more data.
  3. Coaches and families value data → premium conversion.
  4. Revenue fuels growth.

Methodology & sources

  • Games: 2.8–5.3M (8U–18U) with umpires.
  • Coverage = % using free audio. Access = both teams' coaches, players, families.
  • Conversion = % purchasing premium (last 10 pitches free; full data premium).
  • Revenue from premium data.

Sources: Aspen Project Play, NFHS, TeamSnap, GameChanger, SportsEngine, youth sports software research.

APPENDIX A: Market Analysis

3-Year Phased Geographic Rollout

Unit Economics - The Foundation

The League Structure

1 average league: 4 age divisions × 4 teams/division × 12 players/team = 192 players

Revenue per league: 192 players × $199/year = $38,208 ARR

Scaling Math

Target calculation: $25M ÷ $38,208 = 654 leagues needed

Geographic breakdown: 654 leagues ÷ 7 leagues per county = 94 counties

Market penetration: Only 3% of 4.5M serviceable market

3-Year Phased Geographic Rollout

Year 1: California

~58 counties

Pilot & expansion in Northern & Southern CA. Establish repeatable GTM playbook.

Year 2: Texas & Florida

~321 counties

Leverage CA playbook for largest markets. Texas alone: 1,700+ potential leagues.

Year 3: NY, IL & National

Complete top 5 states

National outreach. Top 3 states: 2,600+ potential leagues (4x target).
