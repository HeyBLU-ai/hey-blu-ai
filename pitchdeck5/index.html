Our Technology

We solved four core challenges to make professional-grade AI accessible on any field.

🧠

Proprietary Vision AI

Trained on our exclusive dataset of 1M+ frames, captured at dozens of amateur fields in a wide variety of real-world conditions.

🛰️

Single-Camera 3D Analysis

Our algorithms reconstruct the pitch's full 3D path using monocular depth estimation—allowing a safe, offset camera placement.

🔬

Intelligent Filtering

A physics-aware engine validates every trajectory to eliminate false positives like catcher throw-backs or batted balls.

🎯

Adaptive Strike Zone

Real-time pose estimation adjusts the strike zone for each batter's unique stance.
