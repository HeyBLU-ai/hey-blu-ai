The Problem

According to the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS), nearly 80% of officials quit within their first two years, largely due to verbal abuse from parents and coaches. This trend is creating a critical shortage across all youth sports.

❌

Game Cancellations

Leagues are increasingly forced to cancel or reschedule games due to a lack of certified umpires.

⚠️

Inconsistent Officiating

Parents and coaches with no training are often forced to officiate, leading to inconsistent strike zones and rising on-field disputes.

🔄

High Turnover

The high-pressure, low-support environment makes it nearly impossible to recruit and retain new umpires, worsening the cycle.

4/11

The Solution

HeyBLU is an AI-powered iPhone app that supports umpires with real-time ball-and-strike calls delivered discreetly to an earbud. The umpire remains in complete control, backed by objective data.

⚡

Instant & Objective

On-device AI delivers calls in real time, removing human bias from the most contentious part of the game.

🚀

Effortless Setup

Fast setup with a repeatable placement and per-batter calibration flow.

👥

A Tool to Recruit & Retain Umpires

By reducing conflict and providing a powerful, objective training tool, BLU addresses the core reasons why officials quit.

Key Features & Technology

  • •
    Adaptive Strike Zone: Automatically adjusts for each batter's height and stance.
  • •
    On-Device Processing: Core ball/strike calls run on the iPhone; post-game, premium analytics like heatmaps can sync to the cloud.
  • •
    Repeatable Setup: Standardized placement and per-batter calibration enable consistent behavior across venues and iPhone models.

Independent Validation (Q1 2026)

  • •
    Method: Blinded review vs. certified umpire panel.
  • •
    Endpoints: Agreement %, within-1″ precision, decision-time distribution (by iPhone model), documented failure modes.
  • •
    Settings: Multiple fields under daytime conditions; target N pitches adequate for statistical power.
  • •
    Deliverable: Concise report with representative clips.
