3. Auto-Renewable Subscriptions (HeyBLU Pro)

HeyBLU offers a free tier and an optional paid subscription called HeyBLU Pro. Payment is processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

Subscription options

  • HeyBLU Pro — Monthly: $4.99 USD per month, billed monthly (includes a 14-day free trial when offered)
  • HeyBLU Pro — Annual: $49.99 USD per year, billed annually (includes a 14-day free trial when offered)

HeyBLU Pro is offered at an introductory launch rate. Standard pricing may apply to new subscribers in the future. Prices may vary by country or region based on Apple's App Store pricing tiers. The price shown in the App Store at the time of purchase is the price that applies to your transaction.

Free trial and promotional offers

HeyBLU Pro is offered with a 14-day free trial on monthly and annual plans through Apple's In-App Purchase system. The App Store and the in-app purchase screen will show the trial length and the price that applies after the trial ends.

Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial ends, your subscription automatically converts to a paid plan at the price and billing period you selected (monthly or annual). You will be charged through your Apple ID account when the trial period ends. During a trial, you may cancel at any time in your Apple account settings to avoid being charged.

Introductory or promotional subscription pricing may change over time and is always subject to what Apple displays at purchase.

Auto-renewal

Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase (or at the end of any free trial, if applicable). Your subscription automatically renews unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.

Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period at the then-current subscription rate. Any unused portion of a free trial period is forfeited when you purchase a subscription, where applicable.

How to cancel or manage your subscription

You can manage or cancel HeyBLU Pro at any time through your Apple account:

  • On iPhone or iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → HeyBLU → Cancel Subscription
  • On Mac (macOS Ventura or later): System Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions
  • Online: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

Deleting the HeyBLU app does not cancel your subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You will retain Pro access until that period ends.

Refunds

Subscription purchases and renewals are handled by Apple. Refund requests must be submitted to Apple in accordance with Apple's policies. We cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases directly.

Pro features

HeyBLU (free) includes Follow Game live viewing on a second device (watch-only, no control of the primary phone) and the ability to share a heatmap from the Follow Game session report. Free access does not include Track Pitches on the primary device, Session History, Command Center, or paid export features.

HeyBLU Pro includes Track Pitches (live ball/strike audio calls, estimated pitch speed, and on-screen pitch location), Session History (saved sessions you can revisit), Command Center (Command Training, Calibrate, zone resize, and remote session control from a second device), unlimited pitchers per session, Multi-Pitcher Reports with Every Pitch PDF export, Command Reports, CSV data export, and related coaching tools. We may update which features are included in each tier from time to time.

Follow Game

Follow Game lets a second device watch a live session over your local network at no charge. Follow Game data exists only for the active connection—it is not saved to Session History or retained after you disconnect or close the follower device unless the follower device has its own HeyBLU Pro subscription. Track Pitches, saving sessions to History, and most exports require HeyBLU Pro on the primary tracking device.
