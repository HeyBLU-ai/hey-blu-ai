What you can use today

Track Game — bullpen or live session

Mount an iPhone on a tripod in foul territory. Scan home plate, walk to the mount, verify alignment, and hear live ball/strike calls on every pitch the hitter takes. Choose bullpen or live game mode at session start. Unlimited live audio on Free; Pro saves history, heatmaps, pitch logs, and CSV export.

Command Training (Calibrate BLU)

On a second phone or iPad, open Calibrate BLU (password path). Set intent—zone cell and pitch type—before the rep. HeyBLU compares intent to where the ball actually crossed. Best coach workflow: tripod phone tracks; you hold the calibrator and run the drill.

Pitcher roster (on-device)

Add names to your roster and tap to switch the active pitcher mid-session. Session reports and exports tag pitches to the selected arm—ideal for academy rotations and multi-pitcher bullpens today.

Follow Game — watch from the dugout or bullpen

A second device mirrors live pitch events over local Wi‑Fi—no AR required. Monitor a reliever warming up or share the zone view with a parent without crowding the tripod.

Session reports & Baseball Cards

Pro unlocks shareable graphics and pitch logs you can text between lessons—objective proof of work instead of “felt good in the pen.”
