What you can use today

Live pitch tracking — your bullpen umpire

Live ball/strike feedback during practice—audio on every taken pitch, location on screen. Choose Bullpen (full audio) or Live at bats (hitters in the box). Included with your HeyBLU subscription.

Command Training — intent vs. result

On a second iPhone or iPad, open Command Center and set target zone and pitch type before each rep—same subscription. HeyBLU scores whether the pitch hit your intent—then build a Command Report after the session.

Pitcher roster — switch arms mid-pen

Add names and tap to switch the active pitcher during any session—built for academy rotations, with per-player reports and exports.

Follow Game — watch from the dugout or bullpen

A second device mirrors live calls and the zone—watch only. If a subscriber shares their live feed, non-subscribers with HeyBLU on their own iPhone can Follow Game.

Session reports & Command Reports

Save sessions to History—share session report images, Command Reports (when you ran Command Training), and download a spreadsheet with detailed pitch data for post-lesson review.
