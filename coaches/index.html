What you can use today

Track Game — your bullpen umpire

Mount an iPhone behind the plate and get live ball/strike feedback during practice. At session start, choose Bullpen (full audio) or Live at bats (hitters in the box—strikes announced, every crossing on screen).
Free: live calls, pitch speed, shareable heatmap, one pitcher. HeyBLU Pro: Session History, unlimited pitchers, Every Pitch PDF, and CSV.

Command Training — intent vs. result

On a second iPhone or iPad, open Command Center and set target zone and pitch type before each rep. HeyBLU scores whether the pitch hit your intent—then build a Command Report after the session. Requires HeyBLU Pro on the primary tracking device.

Pitcher roster — switch arms mid-pen

Add names and tap to switch the active pitcher during any session—built for academy rotations. Free: one pitcher per session. HeyBLU Pro: unlimited pitchers with per-player reports and exports.

Follow Game — watch from the dugout or bullpen

A second device mirrors live calls and zone view over Wi‑Fi—no AR, watch only, no control of the primary device. Use Android hotspot or travel router when possible. See two-device Wi‑Fi guide.

Session reports & Command Reports (HeyBLU Pro)

Free: share a session heatmap image while the session is active. HeyBLU Pro: save sessions to History, export Every Pitch PDF, Command Reports (when you ran Command Training), and CSV for post-lesson review.
