Market Opportunity — Built Around Free for Umpires

We grow by giving the app away to umpires. Audio calls (ball/strike) are free; families and leagues pay for premium visualizations (per‑pitch location heatmaps, MPH) and reports. Umpire fees are unchanged.

Total Addressable Market (TAM)

$50B+

U.S. organized youth sports household spend (context for ability to pay).

[A] Aspen Project Play; other market snapshots.

Serviceable Addressable (SAM)

≈$1.2B

Youth sports software/apps (management, stats, analytics). Our wedge is per‑pitch diamond data.

[B] Market research on youth sports software/apps.

Serviceable Obtainable (SOM)

$150–300M

Initial obtainable spend from premium visuals & per‑pitch reports purchased by families/leagues; no change to umpire fees.

[C] Bottom‑up model below.

Why this scales fast

  • Free for umpires → rapid adoption at the point of play.
  • Solves the hardest task (ball/strike) with audio only.
  • Each pitch produces shareable data exhaust families value.

Validated ability to pay

Households already spend $1,016 per child per year on a primary sport; a $99–$199 digital add‑on is a small, proven upsell.

[D] Aspen Project Play 2024/25 survey.

Why now

  • Persistent youth umpire shortages; tech assists recruitment/retention.
  • Normalization of AI in amateur sports (video, stats, wearables).
  • Record youth sports participation; mobile‑first workflows.

Positioning

"Umpire co‑pilot" for accuracy & confidence (free), unlocking premium visuals & diamond‑data products for families and leagues (paid). We do not change or replace umpire fees.

Adoption flywheel

  1. Free co‑pilot reduces stress & abuse for umpires → supply grows.
  2. More covered games → more per‑pitch data exhaust.
  3. Families want visuals & MPH → premium conversion.
  4. Revenue funds further umpire recruitment & product quality.
