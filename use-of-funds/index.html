Tranche B: $750,000

Total Funding Structure: Up to $2 million across three independent, milestone-driven tranches over approximately 33 months. Each tranche is independent, meaning we proceed to the next tranche only upon achieving specific performance targets. The first two tranches ($1 million total) fund us through product-market fit and initial revenue generation. Tranche C provides additional capital for scaling once we've validated our business model.

Product Phases:

MVP (Umpire): Onboard umpires with a free, high-value data-capture tool.

Pilot (Coach/Parent): Launch a freemium analytics app to monetize our user base.

Scale (Organization): Secure B2B contracts with leagues and districts for sustainable, long-term revenue.

2. Product & Growth Phases

Our growth model is designed to build a strong data foundation and user base before scaling monetization.

Phase 1: Umpire MVP & Pilot (Tranche A)

Product: A free mobile application for umpires that captures officiating data and audio.

Value: Provides umpires with tools for review, training, and validation. For HeyBLU, it builds our core data asset and a direct channel to leagues.

Goal: Establish a strong foothold in pilot counties, prove product utility, and gather data.

Phase 2: Coach & Parent App (Tranche B)

Product: A freemium analytics application for coaches and parents, with paid tiers unlocking advanced insights, player metrics, and game analysis.

Pricing: $99/season or $199/year.

Goal: Achieve product-market fit, validate conversion models (Target: 2% parents, 10% coaches), and build our first significant revenue stream.

Phase 3: Organization & League Sales (Tranche C)

Product: A customized B2B platform for leagues, districts, and organizations.

Pricing: Custom annual contracts based on league size and features.

Goal: Secure long-term, high-value ARR, establish HeyBLU as essential infrastructure for youth sports, and drive toward profitability.

3. Funding Tranches & Milestones

This funding structure is organized across three tranches, each tied to performance milestones. The detailed Excel model shows exactly how each dollar would be allocated during each tranche's duration, ensuring transparency and accountability. The first two tranches ($1 million total) fund us through product-market fit and initial revenue generation. Tranche C provides additional capital for scaling operations once we've validated our business model and achieved key milestones. The "Surplus/Deficit" row in the spreadsheet indicates any buffer or adjustment needed to align allocations with the tranche amounts.

Tranche A: $250,000 (MVP & Pilot)

Primary Use: Fund the core team (Founders, 2 Engineers) to build and launch the Umpire MVP. Secure initial league partnerships and run successful pilots.

Key Milestones:

  • Umpire MVP application complete.
  • 100+ active umpires onboarded.
  • 5+ pilot leagues secured and active.

Tranche B: $750,000 (Product-Market Fit)

Trigger: Successful completion of Tranche A milestones and positive engagement data from pilots.

Primary Use: Develop and launch the Coach/Parent freemium app. Expand the team with initial sales and marketing hires. Scale to 50+ leagues.

Key Milestones:

  • Achieve 2% parent and 10% coach free-to-paid conversion rates.
  • Reach $15,000 in Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).
  • Validate unit economics (LTV/CAC > 3:1).

Tranche C: $1,000,000 (Scalable Growth)

Trigger: Reaching Tranche B MRR and conversion milestones.

Primary Use: Scale the organization-level sales team. Invest in data science to enhance product value. Expand marketing for a national footprint.

Key Milestones:

  • Achieve $100,000 in MRR.
  • Secure 10+ multi-league organization or district contracts.
  • Establish a repeatable, scalable sales process.

5. Hiring & Compensation Plan

Philosophy: We are building a lean, expert team, aligning compensation directly with company performance and milestones.

Core Team (Tranche A)

2 Founders, 2 Engineers.

Expansion Team (Tranche B/C)

We will add talent methodically in Sales (Org Sales, Partner Managers), Marketing, Customer Support, and Data Science as we achieve revenue and product milestones.

Founder Compensation

Founder compensation is structured to align with company milestones while prioritizing capital efficiency and growth. We scale our pay modestly as we achieve key revenue targets, demonstrating our commitment to building a sustainable, capital-efficient business.

Stage 1 (Tranche A - Current Pay)

$7,500 per month (each), or $90,000 annually. This is our current compensation level, maintained during the MVP and pilot phase while we validate product-market fit.

Stage 2 (Tranche B & C - Triggers at $10k MRR)

$10,000 per month (each), or $120,000 annually. This increase occurs when we hit $10,000 in Monthly Recurring Revenue, demonstrating product-market fit. We maintain this compensation level through Tranche C, ensuring maximum capital is allocated to Engineers, sales, marketing, and growth initiatives that drive sustainable scaling.

Capital Efficiency Focus: By keeping founder compensation at $120,000 annually through all growth stages, we ensure that every dollar of funding is optimized for company growth. This approach demonstrates our alignment with investor interests and our commitment to building a capital-efficient, scalable business.

How to Read the Financial Model

The Excel spreadsheet provides a detailed, line-item breakdown of how funds are allocated across each tranche. Here's how to interpret it:

Time-Bound Allocations: Each tranche column (A, B, C) shows costs incurred only during that tranche's specific time period. For example, Tranche C founders' pay ($240k) represents 12 months of Stage 2 compensation ($120k annual × 2 founders × 12/12 months).

Total Alignment: The "Total Allocation" row (row 19) sums all costs across all three tranches. The "Tranche Amount" row (row 20) shows the total funding ($2M). The "Surplus/Deficit" row (row 21) indicates whether allocations match the total raise—a small surplus provides a buffer, while a deficit would require adjustments.

Why These Two Documents Work Together: This web page provides the strategic narrative—what we're building, why, and when. The Excel model provides the financial precision—exactly how each dollar is allocated during each time period. Together, they tell a complete, transparent story of capital efficiency and disciplined growth.

Note: The model is designed to be interactive—you can adjust assumptions in the "Assumptions" sheet to see how changes impact the overall allocation and runway.
