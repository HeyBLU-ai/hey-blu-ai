1. Executive Summary

This plan outlines the $2 million strategic investment to launch and scale BLU, establishing it as the new standard for youth sports officiating and analytics. The funding is structured in three tranches, each tied to clear performance milestones. This approach enables disciplined growth through three distinct product phases:

MVP (Umpire): Onboard umpires with a free, high-value data-capture tool.

Pilot (Coach/Parent): Launch a freemium analytics app to monetize our user base.

Scale (Organization): Secure B2B contracts with leagues and districts for sustainable, long-term revenue.

This raise provides 24-30 months of runway, allowing us to achieve significant market penetration and a strong position for future growth.

2. Product & Growth Phases

Our growth model is designed to build a strong data foundation and user base before scaling monetization.

Phase 1: Umpire MVP & Pilot (Tranche A)

Product: A free mobile application for umpires that captures officiating data and audio.

Value: Provides umpires with tools for review, training, and validation. For BLU, it builds our core data asset and a direct channel to leagues.

Goal: Establish a strong foothold in pilot counties, prove product utility, and gather data.

Phase 2: Coach & Parent App (Tranche B)

Product: A freemium analytics application for coaches and parents, with paid tiers unlocking advanced insights, player metrics, and game analysis.

Pricing: $99/season or $199/year.

Goal: Achieve product-market fit, validate conversion models (Target: 2% parents, 10% coaches), and build our first significant revenue stream.

Phase 3: Organization & League Sales (Tranche C)

Product: A customized B2B platform for leagues, districts, and organizations.

Pricing: Custom annual contracts based on league size and features.

Goal: Secure long-term, high-value ARR, establish BLU as essential infrastructure for youth sports, and drive toward profitability.

3. Funding Tranches & Milestones

This $2 million raise is allocated across three tranches to align capital with performance.

Tranche A: $250,000 (MVP & Pilot)

Primary Use: Fund the core team (Founders, 2 Engineers) to build and launch the Umpire MVP. Secure initial league partnerships and run successful pilots.

Key Milestones:

  • Umpire MVP application complete.
  • 100+ active umpires onboarded.
  • 5+ pilot leagues secured and active.

Tranche B: $750,000 (Product-Market Fit)

Trigger: Successful completion of Tranche A milestones and positive engagement data from pilots.

Primary Use: Develop and launch the Coach/Parent freemium app. Expand the team with initial sales and marketing hires. Scale to 50+ leagues.

Key Milestones:

  • Achieve 2% parent and 10% coach free-to-paid conversion rates.
  • Reach $15,000 in Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).
  • Validate unit economics (LTV/CAC > 3:1).

Tranche C: $1,000,000 (Scalable Growth)

Trigger: Reaching Tranche B MRR and conversion milestones.

Primary Use: Scale the organization-level sales team. Invest in data science to enhance product value. Expand marketing for a national footprint.

Key Milestones:

  • Achieve $100,000 in MRR.
  • Secure 10+ multi-league organization or district contracts.
  • Establish a repeatable, scalable sales process.

4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

We will measure success through a focused set of metrics:

Acquisition

Umpires Onboarded
New Leagues Acquired
App Downloads

Activation

Percentage of Active Umpires
Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate (Parent/Coach)

Revenue

Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
Blended Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)

Economics

LTV to CAC Ratio (Target 3:1+)
Payback Period (Target < 12 months)
Gross Margin (Target 55%+)

5. Hiring & Compensation Plan

Philosophy: We are building a lean, expert team, aligning compensation directly with company performance and milestones.

Core Team (Tranche A)

2 Founders, 2 Engineers.

Expansion Team (Tranche B/C)

We will add talent methodically in Sales (Org Sales, Partner Managers), Marketing, Customer Support, and Data Science as we achieve revenue and product milestones.

Founder Compensation

Founder pay is staged to preserve capital and align incentives.

Stage 1 (Tranche A)

$7,500 per month (each), or $90,000 annually. The focus is on product development and efficient capital use.

Stage 2 (Tranche B / $10k MRR)

$10,000 per month (each), or $120,000 annually.

Stage 3 (Tranche C / $50k MRR)

$250,000 per year (each). This aligns founder compensation with significant, sustainable company growth.
