Executive Summary: A B2C Data Company

This plan details the $2 million strategic investment to launch and scale HeyBLU as a dominant B2C data company for youth sports. Our model is simple, sharp, and focused on our Umpire-as-Sensor strategy:

  1. Acquire Umpires (Our Sensors): We provide a 100% free, high-value ball/strike calling app to umpires. This is our "sensor network" and our primary marketing and acquisition cost.
  2. Viral Distribution (K-Factor): One umpire using our app in a game virally exposes the HeyBLU platform to 25+ players and their associated coaches and parents—our *actual* customers. This is our viral loop.
  3. Monetize B2C Users (Our Data Product): We convert this virally-acquired audience of parents and coaches from a freemium app to a paid subscription ($199/yr) that unlocks the pitch location data, analytics, and heat maps. This is our B2C data product.

This raise provides 24-30 months of runway to prove this B2C-only model: acquire a critical mass of umpires, validate our conversion funnel, and scale the B2C engine.

Product & Growth Phases: A B2C Data Strategy

Our growth strategy centers on the Umpire-as-Sensor model, where umpires are our data collection asset and viral distribution channel. The viral loop connects umpires → players → parents/coaches, creating our B2C data product opportunity.

Phase 1: Umpire Adoption (Our Sensor Network)

Product: A free mobile application for umpires that accurately calls balls and strikes using audio data.
Value: For umpires, it's a tool for in-game assistance, training, and reducing sideline conflict. For HeyBLU, this is our data collection asset and our single most important marketing channel—our "sensor network."
Goal: Achieve mass adoption by umpires. Our primary cost in this phase is marketing and partnerships to get this free tool into the hands of 1,000+ umpires.

Phase 2: B2C Product-Market Fit (The Data Product)

Product: A freemium analytics application for parents and coaches, distributed virally during games. The paid tiers unlock the valuable pitch location data, player heat maps, and historical analytics.
Goal: Prove that a percentage of virally-acquired free users will pay for this data. This phase is entirely focused on optimizing the freemium-to-paid conversion funnel and validating our LTV/CAC. The viral loop is our primary distribution mechanism.

Phase 3: Scaling the B2C Engine

Product: Advanced data features, comparative analytics, and social sharing tools to increase the value of the paid subscription.
Goal: Pour fuel on the fire. With a proven B2C funnel, this phase is about scaling the two levers that matter:

  1. Minimize Umpire CAC: Drive down the cost of acquiring one active umpire (our 'sensor').
  2. Maximize LTV: Improve the B2C conversion rate and increase the lifetime value of each paying parent/coach.
This is a marketing and product-led scale-up, not a sales-led one.

Funding Tranches & Milestones

This $2M raise is allocated across three tranches, aligning capital with performance in our B2C-only model.

Tranche A: $250,000 (MVP & Sensor Network)

  • Primary Use: Fund the core team (Founders, 2 Engineers) to build and launch the Umpire MVP and the freemium Parent/Coach App.
  • Key Milestones:
    • Umpire MVP application complete and live.
    • 100+ active umpires onboarded (our first 100 "sensors").
    • 5+ league partnerships active (for viral distribution, not sales).
    • Generate $3-5k MRR from early adopters.

Tranche B: $750,000 (B2C Product-Market Fit)

  • Trigger: Successful completion of Tranche A milestones.
  • Primary Use: Fund growth marketing to acquire more umpires. A/B test and optimize the B2C conversion funnel.
  • Key Milestones:
    • Achieve 2% parent and 3% coach free-to-paid conversion rates.
    • Reach $35,000 in stable Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).
    • Validate unit economics (LTV/CAC > 3:1).

Tranche C: $1,000,000 (Scaling the B2C Engine)

  • Trigger: Reaching Tranche B MRR and conversion milestones.
  • Primary Use: Scale the Umpire acquisition marketing budget. Expand the team with growth marketers and community managers.
  • Key Milestones:
    • Achieve $100,000 in MRR.
    • Scale active umpire network to 5,000+.
    • Establish a positive, blended Umpire-to-Paid-User ratio (1 Umpire = 1+ Paying Customer).

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Our focus is on a few core B2C metrics. We will live and die by these numbers.

  • Umpire Acquisition Cost (U-CAC): Our primary "cost of goods" for data.
  • Active Umpire Count: The size of our "sensor network."
  • Umpire-to-Freemium-User Ratio (K-Factor): Measures the efficiency of our viral loop.
  • Freemium-to-Paid Conversion Rate: The efficiency of our monetization.
  • Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): The ultimate measure of B2C product-market fit.
  • LTV / CAC Ratio: The long-term health of the business.

Hiring & Compensation Plan

Our hiring plan reflects our B2C-only focus. We are building a lean, expert team in product and growth, not sales.

  • Core Team (Tranche A): 2 Founders, 2 Engineers.
  • Expansion Team (Tranche B/C): We will add talent methodically in **Growth & Partnerships** (Community Managers for umpire outreach, Growth Marketers for B2C funnel optimization) and **Product** (Data Science, UI/UX).
  • Founder Compensation: Founder pay is staged to preserve capital and aligns directly with performance milestones, as detailed in the "Pay_Triggers" tab of the model.

How to Read the Connected Financial Model

This web page is the "why" (the narrative). The linked Excel file is the "how" (the numbers). The Excel model has been updated to reflect this B2C-only strategy, with a few key tabs to note:

The "Funnel_Model" Tab:
This is the engine of the entire spreadsheet. (It replaces the old "County_League_Model" tab). It projects a 3-year forecast starting with "New Umpires Acquired" as the main input (our "sensor network"). From that, it calculates "Total Freemium Users Reached" (our viral loop) and then "Total ARR" based on our B2C conversion rates. All other tabs pull their data from this Funnel_Model engine. This is the core of our B2C plan.

The "Tranche_Detail" Tab:
This tab allocates the $2M across Tranches A (MVP), B (B2C Fit), and C (B2C Scale). It reflects hiring plans for engineering and Growth & Partnerships (not sales). The "CAC - Organization" budget line is $0 for all tranches. The "CAC - Ambassador" and "CAC - Social/Digital" budget lines are dynamic formulas that calculate costs based on the number of new B2C users from the Funnel_Model multiplied by the CAC price from the Assumptions tab. This ensures our S&M spending scales directly with user acquisition.

The "Cash_Runway" Tab:
This tab shows our monthly cash flow, net burn, and remaining runway. It is linked to all our assumptions and proves this $2M raise provides 24-30 months of runway to hit our B2C milestones.

The "Unit_Economics" Tab:
This breaks down the LTV, CAC, and Payback Period for our two core paying customers: **Parents and Coaches.**

Why These Two Documents Work Together: This web page provides the strategic narrative—what we're building, why, and when. The Excel model provides the financial precision—exactly how we will acquire users and how each dollar is allocated. Together, they tell a complete, transparent story of capital efficiency and disciplined B2C growth.

Note: The model is interactive—adjust inputs in the "Assumptions" tab to see how changes impact the B2C-only model.
