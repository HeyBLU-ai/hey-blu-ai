Executive Summary: A B2C Data Company

This plan outlines a $2 million strategic investment to launch and scale HeyBLU as a B2C data company for youth sports. Our model operates through three interconnected components:

  1. Umpire Acquisition: We provide a free ball/strike calling app to umpires, creating our primary data collection and distribution channel.
  2. Viral Distribution: Each umpire using our app exposes the platform to 25+ players, coaches, and parents during games—our target customer base.
  3. B2C Monetization: We convert this virally-acquired audience to paid subscriptions ($199/yr) that unlock pitch location data, analytics, and heat maps.

This raise provides 24-30 months of runway to acquire a critical mass of umpires, validate our conversion funnel, and scale the B2C engine.

Product & Growth Phases

Our growth strategy operates through a three-phase progression that leverages umpires as both data collection assets and viral distribution channels.

Phase 1: Umpire Adoption

Product: A free mobile application for umpires that accurately calls balls and strikes using audio data, providing in-game assistance and training value.
Goal: Achieve mass adoption by 1,000+ umpires through marketing and partnerships. This establishes our data collection and distribution infrastructure.

Phase 2: B2C Product-Market Fit

Product: A freemium analytics application for parents and coaches, distributed during games. Paid tiers unlock pitch location data, player heat maps, and historical analytics.
Goal: Optimize the freemium-to-paid conversion funnel and validate unit economics (LTV/CAC > 3:1). This phase focuses entirely on proving the B2C monetization model.

Phase 3: Scaling the B2C Engine

Product: Advanced data features, comparative analytics, and social sharing tools to increase subscription value.
Goal: Scale through two primary levers: minimizing umpire acquisition cost and maximizing lifetime value of paying users. This is a marketing and product-led scale-up.

Funding Tranches & Milestones

This $2M raise is allocated across three tranches, aligning capital with performance in our B2C-only model.

Tranche A: $250,000 (MVP Launch)

  • Primary Use: Fund the core team (Founders, 2 Engineers) to build and launch the Umpire MVP and freemium Parent/Coach App.
  • Key Milestones:
    • Umpire MVP application complete and live.
    • 100+ active umpires onboarded.
    • 5+ league partnerships active.
    • Generate $3-5k MRR from early adopters.

Tranche B: $750,000 (B2C Product-Market Fit)

  • Trigger: Successful completion of Tranche A milestones.
  • Primary Use: Fund growth marketing to acquire more umpires. A/B test and optimize the B2C conversion funnel.
  • Key Milestones:
    • Achieve 2% parent and 3% coach free-to-paid conversion rates.
    • Reach $35,000 in stable Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).
    • Validate unit economics (LTV/CAC > 3:1).

Tranche C: $1,000,000 (Scaling the B2C Engine)

  • Trigger: Reaching Tranche B MRR and conversion milestones.
  • Primary Use: Scale the Umpire acquisition marketing budget. Expand the team with growth marketers and community managers.
  • Key Milestones:
    • Achieve $100,000 in MRR.
    • Scale active umpire network to 5,000+.
    • Establish a positive, blended Umpire-to-Paid-User ratio (1 Umpire = 1+ Paying Customer).

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

We track core B2C metrics that drive our business model:

  • Umpire Acquisition Cost (U-CAC): Cost to acquire each active umpire.
  • Active Umpire Count: Size of our distribution network.
  • Umpire-to-Freemium-User Ratio (K-Factor): Viral distribution efficiency.
  • Freemium-to-Paid Conversion Rate: Monetization efficiency.
  • Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): Primary measure of product-market fit.
  • LTV / CAC Ratio: Long-term business health indicator.

Hiring & Compensation Plan

Our hiring plan reflects our B2C-only focus. We are building a lean, expert team in product and growth, not sales.

  • Core Team (Tranche A): 2 Founders, 2 Engineers.
  • Expansion Team (Tranche B/C): We will add talent methodically in **Growth & Partnerships** (Community Managers for umpire outreach, Growth Marketers for B2C funnel optimization) and **Product** (Data Science, UI/UX).
  • Founder Compensation: Founder pay is staged to preserve capital and aligns directly with performance milestones, as detailed in the "Pay_Triggers" tab of the model.

How to Read the Financial Model

This page provides the strategic narrative; the Excel file contains the detailed financial projections. Key tabs to review:

The "Funnel_Model" Tab:
This is the engine of the entire spreadsheet. It projects a 3-year forecast starting with "New Umpires Acquired" as the main input, calculates "Total Freemium Users Reached" through viral distribution, and then "Total ARR" based on B2C conversion rates. All other tabs pull their data from this Funnel_Model.

The "Tranche_Detail" Tab:
Allocates the $2M across Tranches A (MVP), B (B2C Fit), and C (B2C Scale). Reflects hiring plans for engineering and Growth & Partnerships teams. The "CAC - Ambassador" and "CAC - Social/Digital" budget lines are dynamic formulas that scale costs based on new B2C users from the Funnel_Model.

The "Cash_Runway" Tab:
Shows monthly cash flow, net burn, and remaining runway. Demonstrates that this $2M raise provides 24-30 months to hit our B2C milestones.

The "Unit_Economics" Tab:
Breaks down LTV, CAC, and Payback Period for Parents and Coaches—our two core paying customer segments.

Why These Documents Work Together: This page outlines what we're building and why; the Excel model provides financial precision showing exactly how we acquire users and allocate capital. Together, they tell a complete story of capital efficiency and disciplined B2C growth.
