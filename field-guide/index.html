HeyBLU

First-Time Field Guide

"ABS" (Automated Balls & Strikes) requires user calibration. The iPhone needs to study the area around home plate. It only works if you set it up right. Bad setup = bad calls.

1. What to Bring
  • Tripod or Fence Mount Hand-holding shakes the view and ruins calls. The phone must stay still.
  • Bluetooth Speaker The phone's speaker is too quiet. Pitchers need to hear calls from the mound.
  • Patience for Setup Rushing the field scan is the #1 reason sessions break.
2. The "Scan" (Mapping)

Your phone is building a 3D mental map. You are not recording yet—you are showing the app around.

  • Move slowly: Smooth, short arcs. No fast spinning.
  • Keep plate in view: Ensure the real plate is visible. Don't scan empty dirt or sky.
  • Clear the area: Ask players to step away. Walking bodies confuse the 3D map.
3. Place Strikezone

Follow on-screen instructions. Once the blue plate entirely covers the real home plate, hit "Lock" and get ready to mount your iPhone.

4. Mount & Frame Shot
If the app can't see the plate, it can't call the pitch.
1
Position the Mount

Place tripod 12–20 ft behind the plate. If you go past 22 feet, the app will warn you.

Set up on either the 1st Base or 3rd Base side to avoid looking directly into the sun, or to avoid white backgrounds like fence banners. The app will auto-detect which side you are on.

2
Center Plate & Mound

Home plate should be just inside the thin blue line. The mound's blue line just needs to be near it. Equal space behind mound & plate is ideal.

3
Align Blue Plate

Before walking away, if you see white under the blue home plate, use "Adjust Plate" to re-align.

4
Hit Play

Only tap "Play Ball" when the phone is mounted, completely still, and the plate is centered.

Quick Troubleshooting

Even with a perfect setup, things drift. Always trust your eyes.

If this happens... Do this...
Plate shifted off real plate Use the app's "plate adjust/nudge" tool to fix it.
Calls are wildly inaccurate Reposition the tripod closer or rescan if you must.
Not enough strikes? Resize the zone to get the strikes you were expecting.
Missing consistently to one side Tap Gear icon > "Adjust BLU".
"Near" brings calls towards camera (outside to a Righty). "Far" pushes inside.
Nothing is working right Redo the setup. A fresh scan takes 30s and beats fighting a bad session.
Remember: Mount it steady. Center the plate and mound. Scan slowly. If it looks wrong on screen, it is wrong—don't blame the player, adjust the app!
