Fix a problem

If this happens Do this
Second device won’t connect Use Android hotspot or travel router. iPhone hotspot only if Umpire phone: Airplane Mode → Wi‑Fi on → join. Details below
Tracker antenna shows 7/7; another phone won’t join Session full—disconnect an unused second screen, or turn Share Game off and on. See Multiple second screens
No calls / ball not tracked Check red-line framing—full flight between both lines. Camera beyond ~15 ft? Move to 10–12 ft
No calls with a left-handed batter in the box Camera too far. Move to 10–12 ft—batter obstruction won't drop below the 5-detection minimum
Call was 1–2 feet off on a pitch with contact Batted-ball contamination—use Live At Bats mode when hitters are in the box
Pitch card shows DRIFTED Phone position shifted during flight. Run one Adjust Plate or 9-Grid calibration to stabilize
Zone shifted after mount Slide zone · Distance/Height · Adjust Plate (Pro: measured distance + lens height)
Every pitch inside or outside Calibrate (9-zone grid) on a pitch without contact
Tripod bumped or long idle Restart session; rescan if people walked through the scene
Phone hot in sun Cool it; thermal throttle hurts AR
Calibrate after a swing Use a pitch not swung at (Live at bats when hitters are in the box)
Still broken after Calibrate End session and run setup again

MPH is approximate (±5). Strike zone ~within one ball width when setup is good. More detail: Help & FAQ → Troubleshooting.

Setup now

  • Tripod 10–15 ft from the plate, inside the fence, offset to one side.
  • Camera height about mid-zone (between bottom and top of zone)—lowest: bottom of zone; highest: top of zone. See real home plate and the highest expected pitch in frame.
  • Ball path between the two red lines—zone on one line, mound on the other (don’t center the zone between them).
  • Virtual plate on the real plate before Play Ball; fix shift after mount (slide zone, distance/height, Adjust Plate).
  • Every call wrong? Calibrate on a test pitch without a swing.

Connect two devices

Follow Game and Command Center need a network where both iPhones can find each other. Not every Wi‑Fi or hotspot works—iOS Personal Hotspot blocks device-to-device communication by default. Single-phone tracking does not need Wi‑Fi.

What works (easiest first)

Setup Result
Android phone hotspot — both iPhones join (no SIM needed) Recommended field setup
Travel router (~$20, e.g. TP-Link TL-WR802N or GL‑iNet pocket router) Best for 3+ phones—all join; no internet required
3rd-party iPhone hotspot — Umpire phone: Airplane Mode → Wi‑Fi on → join first Works only with that Airplane Mode step on the tracking iPhone

What does not work

Umpire iPhone as its own hotspot Does not work
Two iPhones on same iOS hotspot without Airplane Mode + Wi‑Fi on Umpire phone Does not work

Multiple second screens (experimental)

One tracking iPhone can share live data with up to 7 other devices at once (typical: 1 Command Center + up to 6 Follow Game phones for parents or dugout coaches). Follow Game and Command Center can connect at the same time—you do not need to disconnect one for the other.

  • On the tracker: turn on Share Game (antenna) after every device joins Wi‑Fi. The badge shows connected/7 (e.g. 2/7).
  • 1–2 second screens are field-tested. 3 or more are supported in the app but experimental—use a travel router and report issues to support@heyblu.ai.
  • Use only one Command Center per session for remote control; extra slots are for Follow Game viewers.

On each iPhone: Settings → HeyBLU → Local Network On. Full walkthrough: Help & FAQ → Connect two devices.

Before the field

Before the field
  • Volume up or Bluetooth speaker / earbuds
  • HeyBLU → Local Network On (second device)
  • Two devices? Android hotspot or travel router (recommended; travel router best for 3+ phones). iPhone hotspot: Umpire phone needs Airplane Mode → Wi‑Fi on first
  • Clean camera lens
  • Tripod (inside fence); measuring tape on Pro phones
  • Charge phone; battery pack for long sessions
  • Game balls—slightly scuffed white tracks best

Limits

Works best: outdoor · 10–15 ft · daylight · ~60 MPH and under.

Expect more hassle: indoor only · much past ~15 ft · 65+ MPH untested · battery under ~30% · heavy breaking stuff still in testing.
