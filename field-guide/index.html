Fix a problem

No calls / ball not tracked

Check red-line framing—full flight between both lines. Camera beyond ~15 ft? Move to 10–12 ft.

Inside/outside calls consistently wrong; camera angle was less than ~20° from pitch line

Reposition tripod to ~45° offset from the pitch line — the camera can't judge inside/outside when shooting nearly head-on.

Ball not detected; white fence or light wall behind pitcher

White background makes a white ball invisible — move to the other side of the plate or find a darker backdrop.

No calls (or fewer calls) with a left-handed batter in the box

The batter can block the camera's view of part of the pitch. Shoot from the recommended ~45° angle, not head-on—that keeps most of the flight visible even with a batter in the way.

Calls consistently land 1–2 feet off with a batter in the box

Confirm Live At Bats was selected on the setup screen—Bullpen mode can track the batted ball instead of the pitch. Still off after switching? Calibrate on a clean pitch, or rescan.

Calls drifting on an all-dirt or low-contrast field

Calibrate on a clean pitch to stabilize. Persistent drift: end session, clear the plate area, fresh scan.

Consistent misses to one side or the other?

Tap Calibrate, move the yellow dot to the correct location, and test another pitch.

Calls consistently too high, low, inside, or outside — even after calibrating

A zone that's too small will get more "Ball" calls; one too large will get too many "Strike" calls. Check zone size: gear icon during session, or Resize Zone after session.

Zone doesn't line up with the real plate after mounting

Drag the zone back onto the plate, or tap Adjust Plate for an overhead view and use the arrows to slide it into place. Pro iPhones: enter your measured distance and lens height for the best fit.

Calibrate not improving calls — or Apply button grayed out

Only calibrate on a pitch with no contact. Apply is grayed if there wasn't enough data. Use Live At Bats mode when hitters are in the box.

Still broken after Calibrate

Close the app, clear the plate area, and start with a fresh scan.

Tripod bumped or long idle

Restart session; rescan if people walked through the scene.

Phone hot in sun

Cool it — accuracy drops when the phone overheats.

Second device won't connect

Use Android hotspot or travel router. iPhone hotspot only if Umpire phone: Airplane Mode → Wi‑Fi on → join. Details below.

Tracker antenna shows 7/7; another phone won't join

Session full—disconnect an unused second screen, or turn Share Game off and on. See Multiple second screens.

MPH is approximate (±5). Strike zone ~within one ball width when setup is good. More detail: Help & FAQ → Troubleshooting.

Setup now

  • Tripod 10–15 ft from the plate, offset ~45° from the pitch line (not aimed at the pitcher). Must be mounted—the zone locks to a fixed point; movement after that shifts calls. Narrow spaces that can't allow ~45° offset will limit inside/outside accuracy.
  • Camera height about mid-zone (between bottom and top of zone)—lowest: bottom of zone; highest: top of zone. See real home plate and the highest expected pitch in frame.
  • Ball path between the two red lines—zone on one line, mound on the other (don’t center the zone between them).
  • Virtual plate on the real plate before Play Ball; fix shift after mount (slide zone, distance/height, Adjust Plate).
  • Every call wrong? Calibrate on a test pitch without a swing.

First time? Help & FAQ → First-time setup · tripod height: FAQ · red-line diagram: FAQ.

Connect two devices

Follow Game and Command Center need a network where both iPhones can find each other. Not every Wi‑Fi or hotspot works—iOS Personal Hotspot blocks device-to-device communication by default. Single-phone tracking does not need Wi‑Fi.

What works (easiest first)

Setup Result
Android phone hotspot — both iPhones join (no SIM needed) Recommended field setup
Travel router (~$20, e.g. TP-Link TL-WR802N or GL‑iNet pocket router) Best for 3+ phones—all join; no internet required
3rd-party iPhone hotspot — Umpire phone: Airplane Mode → Wi‑Fi on → join first Works only with that Airplane Mode step on the tracking iPhone

What does not work

Umpire iPhone as its own hotspot Does not work
Two iPhones on same iOS hotspot without Airplane Mode + Wi‑Fi on Umpire phone Does not work

Multiple second screens (experimental)

One tracking iPhone can share live data with up to 7 other devices at once (typical: 1 Command Center + up to 6 Follow Game phones for parents or dugout coaches). Follow Game and Command Center can connect at the same time—you do not need to disconnect one for the other.

  • On the tracker: turn on Share Game (antenna) after every device joins Wi‑Fi. The badge shows connected/7 (e.g. 2/7).
  • 1–2 second screens are field-tested. 3 or more are supported in the app but experimental—use a travel router and report issues to support@heyblu.ai.
  • Use only one Command Center per session for remote control; extra slots are for Follow Game viewers.

On each iPhone: Settings → HeyBLU → Local Network On. Full walkthrough: Help & FAQ → Connect two devices.

Before the field

Before the field
  • Volume up or Bluetooth speaker / earbuds
  • HeyBLU → Local Network On (second device)
  • Two devices? Android hotspot or travel router (recommended; travel router best for 3+ phones). iPhone hotspot: Umpire phone needs Airplane Mode → Wi‑Fi on first
  • Clean camera lens
  • Tripod (inside fence); measuring tape on Pro phones
  • Charge phone; battery pack for long sessions
  • Game balls—slightly scuffed white tracks best

Limits

Works best: outdoor · 10–15 ft · daylight · ~60 MPH and under.

Expect more hassle: indoor only · much past ~15 ft · 65+ MPH untested · battery under ~30% · heavy breaking stuff still in testing.
