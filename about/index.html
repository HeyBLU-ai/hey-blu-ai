About Us

HeyBLU exists because the strike zone has never been consistent, accessible, or easy to measure in practice. We're a team of coaches, players, and builders who believe technology can give pitchers objective feedback, help coaches teach command, and make every bullpen rep count.

Our Story

HeyBLU didn't start in a lab—it started on the field. I've pitched in MSBL for years, and like a lot of players, I was vocal about bad strike zones. My daughter's last baseball at-bat at nine ended on pitches over her head, and the pattern kept repeating across youth games and adult leagues. By early 2023, when AI breakthroughs became mainstream, I found myself asking: "Why can't we solve this with technology?"

A Marin County athletic director later told me their JV teams didn't have umpires because of the shortage. That changed everything. Officials face worsening sportsmanship, low pay, and constant pressure—especially the youngest umpires. Many quit within two seasons. I realized this wasn't just about accuracy; it was about supporting the people keeping the game running.

So we built HeyBLU: an AI-powered bullpen and practice tracker that runs on a single iPhone. It delivers a repeatable strike zone, real-time ball–strike calls during sessions, and pitch-location data coaches can review after every bullpen.

We're building this for coaches, parents, and the pitchers who put in the reps between games.

Brian Marcus

Brian and I have played on the same MSBL teams for over a decade. Brian brings rare sports-tech IP expertise. His firm handled MLB's first strike zone patent. He personally worked on the NFL's yellow first-down marker patent. He has filed all BLU patents and trademarks, and guides our long-term defensibility.

He's also a Little League coach with two sons who grew up playing the game. He understands both sides: the technology, and the parents and coaches who just want fairness on the field.
