About Us

HeyBLU started on the diamond—so every bullpen can have a Big League Umpire.

We exist because the strike zone has never been consistent, accessible, or easy to measure in practice. We're coaches, players, and builders who believe technology can give pitchers objective feedback, help coaches teach command, and make every bullpen rep count—in baseball and fast-pitch softball.

Our Story

On amateur fields, players and coaches rarely know an umpire's name—so they call out "Blue" or "Hey Blue" to get attention, say hello, or ask a question. We took that universal baseball and softball habit and modernized it into HeyBLU, where BLU stands for Big League Umpire.

I'm Rob Regan. HeyBLU didn't start in a lab—it started on the field. I've pitched in MSBL for years, and like a lot of players, I was vocal about bad strike zones. My daughter's last baseball at-bat at nine ended on pitches over her head, and the pattern kept repeating across youth games and adult leagues. By early 2023, when AI breakthroughs became mainstream, I found myself asking: "Why can't we solve this with technology?"

A Marin County athletic director later told me their JV teams didn't have umpires because of the shortage. That changed everything. Officials face worsening sportsmanship, low pay, and constant pressure—especially the youngest umpires. Many quit within two seasons. I realized this wasn't just about accuracy; it was about supporting the people keeping the game running.

So we built HeyBLU: a bullpen and practice tracker that runs on a single iPhone. It delivers a repeatable strike zone, live ball–strike calls during sessions, and pitch-location data coaches can review after every bullpen.

We're building this for coaches, parents, and the pitchers who put in the reps between games.

JP is a 20-year global sales and staffing leader with experience at WeWork, 24 Hour Fitness, Blend, GoPro, and multiple startups, including one he founded and funded himself. He has built revenue teams, hired engineers at scale, and crafted GTM strategies for high-growth companies.

Brian Marcus

Strategic Advisor, IP & Innovation

Brian and I have played on the same MSBL teams for over a decade. When I first started exploring whether a single camera could reliably call balls and strikes, I asked Brian—an IP attorney—if he thought it was even possible. At first, he wasn't convinced. But once he saw our early prototype, he was all in.

Brian brings rare sports-tech IP expertise. His firm handled MLB's first strike zone patent. He personally worked on the NFL's yellow first-down marker patent. He has filed all HeyBLU patents and trademarks, and guides our long-term defensibility.

He's also a Little League coach with two sons who grew up playing the game. He understands both sides: the technology, and the parents and coaches who just want fairness on the field.
